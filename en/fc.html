Background: from FIFA to EA Sports FC

2022. After nearly thirty years together, EA and FIFA failed to agree on renewing the licence. EA kept its licences for clubs, leagues and players.

2023. The series was renamed EA Sports FC, with FC 24 as the first instalment.

A new edition comes out every year at the end of September; FC 27 is the fourth under this name.

Situation as of 11 November 2026.

The game

What's new in FC 27

A new open-world social space, The Grounds, a new gallery in Ultimate Team and a redesigned transfer market in Career Mode.

Ultimate Team

The most played mode: you build your team from player cards, earned by playing or found in packs.

Editions

Standard, Ultimate and Ultimate Plus (early access and bonuses). A free version, FC 27 Lite, lets you try the game.

The publisher

Electronic Arts, whose takeover by an investor group including Saudi Arabia's sovereign fund (PIF) was announced in 2025.

The series

1993 – 2023

The FIFA era

Thirty years of FIFA games, which became the best-selling sports series in the world.

2023 – 2025

FC 24, 25 and 26

The first three instalments under the new name, with women players added to Ultimate Team.

FC 27

Released on 25 September 2026 on all platforms, with The Grounds and a free version.

Controversies and debates

  • Packs — paid Ultimate Team packs are criticised for resembling gambling; Belgium forced the end of FIFA Points sales there in 2019.
  • A game a year — some feel the changes from year to year are too small for a full-price game.
  • Gameplay — every release, tactics seen as overpowered force EA to rebalance the game through updates.

What people say

What players like

  • Official licences for thousands of clubs and players.
  • The new Career Mode features and The Grounds.
  • Quick fixes following player feedback.

What is criticised

  • The role of money in Ultimate Team.
  • Bugs and imbalances at launch.
  • Paying for a new edition every year.
