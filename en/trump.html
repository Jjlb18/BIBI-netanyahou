Background: the 2026 midterm elections

3 November 2026. Republicans lose control of both chambers of Congress. Democrats gain seven Senate seats (a majority of about 54–44) and a large majority in the House of Representatives.

The reasons given. Voters mainly expressed frustration over rising prices, the war with Iran and concerns about corruption.

For the last two years of his term (until January 2029), Trump will face an opposition Congress; he has said he will use his veto against Democratic bills.

Situation as of 11 November 2026 — may change.

Biography

Youth and education

Born in the Queens borough of New York, he is the fourth of five children of Fred Trump, a real-estate developer, and Mary Anne MacLeod, born in Scotland. He attended a military academy and then studied economics at the Wharton School, graduating in 1968.

The businessman

He took over the family business, renamed the Trump Organization, and made his name in Manhattan with Trump Tower (1983). He also invested in casinos, hotels and golf courses, with several business bankruptcies in the 1990s.

Television

From 2004 to 2015 he hosted the reality show The Apprentice, which made him famous with the wider American public.

Family

Married three times (Ivana Zelníčková, Marla Maples, then Melania Knauss in 2005), he has five children: Donald Jr., Ivanka, Eric, Tiffany and Barron.

His terms as president

2017 – 2021

45th president

Elected against Hillary Clinton. 2017 tax cuts, three Supreme Court justices appointed, trade war with China, Abraham Accords. Two impeachments, both ending in Senate acquittals.

2021 – 2025

Between terms

Defeated by Joe Biden, he disputed the result; his supporters stormed the Capitol on 6 January 2021. Several indictments, a conviction in New York (2024) and an assassination attempt in July 2024.

Since 20 Jan 2025

47th president

Elected against Kamala Harris. Sweeping tariffs, deportation policy, strikes on Iran (2025) then war in 2026, Republican defeat in the midterm elections.

Legal cases

Trump is the first former US president to have been criminally indicted. He has always denounced a political “witch hunt”.

  • New York (payments to Stormy Daniels) — convicted on 34 counts of falsifying business records in May 2024; sentenced in January 2025 to an “unconditional discharge”, with no prison time or fine.
  • Federal cases (2020 election and classified documents) — dropped after his 2024 victory, as federal prosecutors do not pursue a sitting president.
  • Georgia (attempt to overturn the 2020 result) — case stalled and then dropped.
  • Civil — found liable in New York for defaming E. Jean Carroll and, in a financial fraud case, ordered to pay a large penalty later overturned on appeal.

A divisive leader

What his supporters highlight

  • Defending American workers and industry through tariffs (“America First”).
  • A firm line on illegal immigration and border security.
  • Tax cuts and deregulation.
  • Power-based diplomacy (Abraham Accords, pressure on Iran).

What his critics hold against him

  • Disputing the 2020 election and his role in the 6 January attack.
  • Pressure on the courts, the media and checks and balances.
  • Rising prices linked to tariffs and the war with Iran.
  • Conflicts of interest and his family's enrichment while in office.
