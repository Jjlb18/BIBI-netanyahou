Biography

Early years

Born in Albuquerque, he was raised by his mother and his Cuban‑born stepfather, Miguel Bezos, who adopted him. He studied computer science and electrical engineering at Princeton.

Wall Street

He worked in finance in New York, notably at the D. E. Shaw fund, before quitting in 1994.

Amazon

He launched an online bookstore near Seattle that grew into a global e‑commerce and cloud (AWS) giant.

Family

Divorced from MacKenzie Scott in 2019, he married Lauren Sánchez in Venice in June 2025. He has four children.

His companies

Since 1994

Amazon

Online retail, AWS cloud, Prime Video. He stepped down as CEO in 2021 and remains executive chairman.

Blue Origin

New Shepard rockets (tourist flights) and New Glenn, which reached orbit and recovered its first stage in November 2025.

Washington Post & AI

Bought the Washington Post in 2013. Since November 2025 he co‑runs Project Prometheus, an AI start‑up with $6.2 billion in funding.

Controversies

His career draws as much admiration as criticism.

  • Working conditions — The pace in Amazon warehouses and opposition to unions are regularly criticised.
  • Washington Post — In 2026, deep layoffs at the paper drew sharp criticism.
  • Taxes — He has argued publicly, including with New York mayor Zohran Mamdani, that taxing billionaires more would not solve problems.
  • Lifestyle — His Venice wedding and yachts have become symbols of inequality for his opponents.

A divisive figure

What admirers highlight

  • Invented an e‑commerce model copied worldwide.
  • AWS runs a large part of the internet.
  • Invests billions in space and climate (Bezos Earth Fund).

What critics point to

  • Tough working conditions at Amazon.
  • Amazon’s dominance over small retailers.
  • Giving seen as small compared with his fortune.
