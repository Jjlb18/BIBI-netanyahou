Background: a hugely anticipated launch

Two delays. Originally planned for autumn 2025, the game was pushed to 26 May 2026, then to 19 November 2026, to allow more polish according to Rockstar.

Pre-orders since 25 June 2026. The standard edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate edition $99.99. The online mode is due separately after launch.

More than thirteen years after GTA V (2013), it is one of the most anticipated games in video game history.

Situation as of 11 November 2026.

The game

The story

Players control a criminal duo, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Lucia is the series' first female lead since it moved to 3D.

The world

Leonida is a fictional state inspired by Florida, with Vice City (inspired by Miami), swamps, beaches and small towns.

The studio

Rockstar Games, part of Take-Two Interactive, also made Red Dead Redemption. GTA 6's development was officially confirmed in February 2022.

Tech

Current-generation consoles only at launch, targeting 30 frames per second.

The series

1997 – 2008

From the top-down first GTA to GTA III (2001), which popularised 3D open worlds, then Vice City, San Andreas and GTA IV.

Controversies and debates

  • Delays — two successive delays frustrated some players.
  • Price — $80 for the standard edition is high for some, although others point out it is cheaper than GTA V once adjusted for inflation.
  • Layoffs — in late 2025 Rockstar fired several dozen staff, citing leaks of confidential information; a UK union called it union-busting.
  • No disc — physical boxes only contain a download code.
  • No PC — no date has been announced for a PC version.

What people say

What excites people

  • A very detailed, lively world, judging by the trailers.
  • The return to Vice City and the duo of protagonists.
  • Rockstar's reputation for quality.

What worries people

  • The price and the lack of a PC version.
  • Only 30 frames per second on consoles.
  • The download size (over 200 GB).
