Biography

Early years

Born in Pretoria, he moved to Canada at 17, then studied physics and economics at the University of Pennsylvania.

First successes

He co‑founded Zip2 (sold in 1999) and then X.com, which became PayPal and was bought by eBay in 2002.

Big bets

With his PayPal money he founded SpaceX (2002) and invested in Tesla, becoming its CEO in 2008.

Family

He has many children from several relationships, including some with the singer Grimes.

His companies

Reusable Falcon and Starship rockets, Starlink satellite internet. It absorbed xAI (the Grok chatbot) and went public in 2026.

Since 2004

Electric cars, batteries, self‑driving and the Cybercab robotaxi. In 2025 shareholders approved a giant pay package for him.
