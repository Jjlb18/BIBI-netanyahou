What is ice hockey?

The rules

Two teams of six players (including a goalie) face off on the ice over three 20‑minute periods. The aim: send the puck into the opposing net with your stick.

If it’s tied

In the NHL, a 5‑minute 3‑on‑3 overtime is played, then a shootout if needed. A win is worth 2 points, an overtime loss 1 point.

A fast, physical sport

Players skate at over 30 km/h and the puck can fly at more than 150 km/h. Body checks are allowed in men’s hockey.

Penalties

A player who breaks the rules goes to the penalty box for 2 minutes or more, and his team plays short‑handed.

Leagues

North America

NHL

The best league in the world: 32 teams in the US and Canada. The champion lifts the Stanley Cup.

Switzerland

National League

The Swiss league, one of Europe’s strongest, with clubs such as Zurich (ZSC Lions), Bern, Fribourg‑Gottéron, Genève‑Servette and Lausanne.

World

Worlds and Olympics

Every spring the World Championship brings the nations together. Switzerland won silver in 2024 and 2025; the 2026 Worlds were held in Switzerland.

Timeline

  • 1875First organised ice hockey game, in Montreal.
  • 1917The NHL is founded.
  • 1920Ice hockey enters the Olympic Games.
  • 1979Wayne Gretzky starts in the NHL; he becomes “The Great One”.
  • 2013Switzerland wins World Championship silver, its first medal since 1953.
  • 2017Nico Hischier becomes the first Swiss No. 1 NHL draft pick.
  • 2025Alexander Ovechkin breaks Gretzky’s goal record (894).
  • 2026World Championship held in Switzerland, in Zurich and Fribourg.

Legends

  • Wayne Gretzky — “The Great One”, all‑time points leader.
  • Mario Lemieux and Sidney Crosby — Penguins legends.
  • Alexander Ovechkin — the NHL’s all‑time leading goal scorer.
  • Connor McDavid — seen as the best player of his generation.

Swiss players in the NHL

  • Roman Josi — defenceman, 2020 Norris Trophy (best defenceman).
  • Nico Hischier — captain of the New Jersey Devils.
  • Timo Meier, Kevin Fiala and Nino Niederreiter — among the best‑known Swiss forwards.
